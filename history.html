When and how was Ikea founded?

Ikea was founded by Ingvar Kamprad, way back in 1943 when he was at the ripe young age of 17. The young business was named after the founder's farm. Before he started selling furniture, he begun his carrer by selling stuff on a smaller scale, like pens and wallets in his home county of Sweden. This business turned into a company when his father gave him a sum of money for his successes in school, which he used to expand his business to furniture, and beyond. Even after his death in 2018, Kamprad's legacy still lives on through the thriving enterprise of Ikea.

How was the company structured?

To make sure the company doesn't fall to corruption, among other reasons, Kamprad decided to split the ownership of the Ikea in a few different sectors.

  • Inter Ikea Systems
    • Owns all the inellectual property, like the logo, brand, trademarks, and so on.
    • Is the franchiser of Ikea.
    • Releases reading material for Ikea, like news, training, and program manuals.
    • Does the research for the market Ikea is in.
  • Ikea of Sweden
    • Designs and develops the furniture for Ikea.
  • Ikea Supply
    • Is reponsible for sending...well, products, to Ikea franchises.
  • Ikea Industry
    • Produces 10-12% of Ikea products.
    • Has a main focus on wooden furniture.
  • Ikea Marketing & Communication
    • Maintains communications between customers and other Ikea organizations.
    • Also is reponsible for maintaining the Ikea Website, Apps, Brochures, TV commericals, instruction manuals, etc.
  • Ikea Food Services.
    • Provides the food for the various resturaunts in the Ikeas.
