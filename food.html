The Glorious Ikea Food

How did these iconic tasty trademarks of Ikea come to be?

How did the meatball recipes come to be?

In 1985, the same year where the iconic Super Mario Brothers game was born, despite the existence of cafes in the Ikeas since the 60s, the business still faced the problem of people not being able to shop due to being hungry. So, with a ton of hard work, the Swedish chef Severin Sjöstedt created a meatball inspired by a traditional Swedish meatball. This meatball took 10 MONTHS to perfect, and the best part! It was affordable, delicious, and most importantly, easy to make!

Meatball Variants

Plenty of variants of these iconic meatballs have popped up over time, like for instance, the chicken teriyaki meatball for those not a fan of red meat. Or the vegetarian meatball, which despite the name, is consumable-friendly for both vegans and vegetarians alike! Or those a fan of fish, salmon meatballs! There's even a MOOSE variant exclusive in the Köket resturaunt in the IKEA Museum made of local hunting game!
