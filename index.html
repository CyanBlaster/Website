What is Ikea?

Ikea is a Swedish furniture store which is around the size of a warehouse. Founded in 1943, They have stores all around the country, including multiple locations in Europe, Austrailia, and North America.

While they do generally sell various types of furniture, Ikea's also famous their iconic Ikea meatballs.

When and how was Ikea founded?

Ikea was founded by Ingvar Kamprad, way back in 1943 when he was at the ripe young age of 17. Before he started selling furniture, he begun his carrer by selling stuff on a smaller scale, like pens and wallets in his home county of Sweden. This business turned into a company when his father gave him a sum of money for his successes in school, which he used to expand his business to furniture, and beyond.
